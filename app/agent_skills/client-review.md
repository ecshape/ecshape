# Client progress review (head coach level)

1. Get the facts: `client_details` (14 days of check-ins, last weigh-ins). Use weekly averages, not single weigh-ins (water swings ±1 kg).
2. Judge the trend vs the goal: fat loss 0.5–1 %/week, gain 0.25–0.5 %/week. Check adherence (check-ins, logged meals, workouts) BEFORE changing the plan.
3. Plateau (2–3 weeks flat with ≥ 80 % adherence): −100–150 kcal or +2,000 steps/day; or +1 set on lagging muscles. Low adherence: simplify (fewer options, prep plan), not cut calories.
4. Red flags (sleep < 6 h, very low energy, pain, disordered-eating signs) → address first; refer to a professional when needed.
5. Feedback message: one win, one specific thing to keep, one change for next week, encouragement. Short, personal, in the client's language.

Output: 3-line summary (trend · adherence · decision) then the ready-to-send message.
