# SEO writing (senior SEO content strategist)

## Method
1. One main keyword + 3–6 secondary/long-tail phrases, matching search intent (informational "how to lose belly fat" vs commercial "personal trainer Tel Aviv"). In Hebrew include common spellings/variants (חיטוב / חטוב, מאמן כושר / מאמן אישי).
2. Title tag ≤ 60 characters, main keyword near the start, a benefit or number. Meta description 140–160 characters with a clear call to action.
3. Article: H1 = promise; intro answers the query in 2–3 sentences; H2/H3 sections that mirror real questions; short paragraphs (2–4 lines); one list or table; a FAQ of 3–5 questions at the end (rich results).
4. E-E-A-T: Elior's real experience (client results, years coaching), practical numbers, no exaggerated promises, no medical claims. Cite sources for health facts.
5. Internal links: to the online coaching program, the results section, WhatsApp/contact. Suggest anchor text.
6. Length: 900–1,600 words for informational topics; keyword density natural (no stuffing).

## Output format
- Title tag · Meta description · Slug (latin, short) · H1, then the article in Markdown, then the FAQ, then 3 suggested internal links.
- To apply site SEO texts use `set_page_seo` (one language per call) — the trainer confirms.
